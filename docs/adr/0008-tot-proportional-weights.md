# Weights in the ToT UM rubric's proportions

Amended by ADR 0009: Built on AWS gets 25 points instead of 20.

Decided by Elite Academy on 16 Oct 2026, superseding the weights in ADR 0006 and 0007. Keeping the 50/50 split after dropping V3 and adding Built on AWS pushed V1 to 21.9% against 10% for each pitch criterion, which looked lopsided. Instead every remaining criterion keeps its share relative to the others as in the ToT UM rubric (each D 10% of the final, V1 17.5%, V2 12.5%, V3 10%, V4 10%), and V3's 10% is spread over the rest in proportion: points 20 per D, 35 V1, 25 V2, 20 V4 out of 180. So 36 × Final = 100 (D1+…+D5) + 175 V1 + 125 V2 + 100 V4, from 25 to 100, still in integers (ADR 0001). Shown weights: D 11.1% each, V1 19.4%, V2 13.9%, V4 11.1%. C1 and C2 remain subtotals for the operator's views only.

V1 is renamed **Clear impact** and asks whether the team explained how the app helps real people; the demo video is not expected to show it.
