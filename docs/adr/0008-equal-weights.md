# All eight criteria weigh the same

Decided by Elite Academy on 16 Oct 2026, superseding the weights in ADR 0006 and 0007. Keeping the ToT UM ratios after dropping V3 and adding Built on AWS left V1 at 21.9% against 10% for each pitch criterion, which looked lopsided, and V1 is judged from the pitch, not from the video. Each of the eight criteria now counts 12.5%: 32 × Final = 100 × (sum of the eight levels), from 25 to 100, still in integers (ADR 0001). C1 and C2 remain subtotals for the operator's views only; they no longer split the final 50/50.

V1 is renamed **Clear impact** and asks whether the team explained how the app helps real people; the demo video is not expected to show it.
