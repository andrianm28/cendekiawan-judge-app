# Ties are decided on the unrounded average

Decided by Elite Academy on 15 Oct 2026. Team finals are shown as whole numbers, but teams are ranked, and ties found, on the exact average of the judges' finals (8 × final summed over judges, compared without division). Before, two finals that both rounded to 77 counted as a tie. A simulation of the event (5 judges × 20 teams, levels mostly 2–4) put a tie among the five category winners at 36–54% of events with rounded finals against 5–9% with the exact average, and a top-of-category tie at 5–9% against about 1%.

The operator's results screen shows the exact average to two decimals beside each rounded final, so the order is explainable on the day. The rest of ADR 0003 stands: an exact tie at the top of a category is picked by that category's judge, and tied winners are ordered by the judges in deliberation.
