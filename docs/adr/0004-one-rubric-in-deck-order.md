# One rubric in the order of the workshop deck

Decided by Elite Academy on 15 Oct 2026, before the rubric was announced to teams. The teams had only seen the Showcase Prep workshop deck (five segments: Problem, Solution, Demo, Real-world impact, Closing), and their demo videos show the app being used, nothing else. A separate "Demo Video" component would have asked judges to find impact (V1) and growth (V4) in a video that was never meant to carry them, and 8 teams had already submitted such videos.

So judges see one rubric, scored from the whole 5-minute slot, with the nine criteria grouped by deck segment (D2 sits under Demo, where the app is seen working). Level texts are the ToT UM descriptors in plain words for non-technical judges, tightened after a four-way independent review on 15 Oct: no overlap between criteria (D2 is the whole flow, V3 is different inputs; D4 is readiness, V4 is reach; D5 is delivery only), use of AI on Amazon Bedrock is named in D2 and D3, and every realistic performance fits exactly one level. Keys, weights and the formula are unchanged (ADR 0001), so a sheet with the same levels gives the same final; C1/C2 survive only as internal subtotals in exports and the operator's views. Rebuilding into eight criteria with round weights was rejected: it would change the tested scoring five days before the event.
