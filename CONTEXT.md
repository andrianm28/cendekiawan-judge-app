# CendekiAwan Judge App: domain glossary

- **Grand Final**: *Grand Finale of AWS CendekiAwan Bedrock AI Solution*, Universiti Malaya, 20 Oct 2026.
- **Category**: one of five competition themes (Smart Education, Digital Health, Sustainable Environment & Climate, Financial Inclusion & SME Empowerment, GovTech & Social Impact).
- **Group**: the showcase block for one category, 4 teams. Group number = category order (1–5). Each **Judge** owns one group's category (for tie-breaks) but scores every team.
- **Judge**: one of five, each on a tablet. Every judge scores all 20 teams; they only score, the operator runs the stage.
- **Running order**: the order teams present in, set by the operator (default = showcase order). The operator puts one team **on stage** at a time and starts its 5-minute **pitch timer**; every judge's tablet opens that team's sheet and shows the same timer.
- **Team**: a finalist; has a team name, an app name, a university (APU, MMU, Nilai, UM) and a showcase **order** (1–20).
- **Score sheet**: one judge's levels (1–4) for a team on D1–D5 (**Solution**, C1) and V1–V4 (**Live Demo**, C2), plus an optional one-sentence note. Status **draft** or **submitted**.
- **Final**: 0.5 × C1 + 0.5 × C2, 25–100, rounded half-up once. Always computed in integers (8 × Final). A team's final is the average of all judges' unrounded finals, rounded once.
- **Category winner**: the highest final in a category. If the top finals are equal once every judge has scored the category, the judge who owns it picks the winner (**tie pick**); D1 and D4 are shown to help, not applied.
- **Closed category**: the operator closes a category; no judge can change those 4 teams' sheets afterwards (operator corrections still allowed, with a reason).
- **Live leaderboard**: all teams ranked overall and per category from the scores in so far, refreshed every few seconds; for the operator laptop or a projector in the judges' room.
- **Proposed rank**: the five category winners ordered by final into Rank 1–5. **Award** names: Rank 1 = Winner of AWS CendekiAwan, Rank 2, Rank 3, Rank 4 = Emerging Innovator, Rank 5 = Rising Innovator.
- **Deliberation**: 12.05–12.35, where judges confirm or reorder the proposed ranks (with a logged reason) and lock the result.
- **MC sheet**: awards in announcement order, Rising → Emerging → Rank 3 → 2 → 1.
- **Operator**: committee member on the laptop who runs the stage (running order, team on stage, pitch timer), tracks every judge's sheets, closes categories and corrects scores.
- **Reset**: after a rehearsal, the operator wipes all event data (scores, tie picks, closed categories, running order, deliberation, change log). Judges, names and PINs stay. A new **epoch** tells tablets to drop their local copies.
